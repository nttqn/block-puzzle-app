How it plays

Drag & fit

Drag any of your 3 blocks onto the grid — fill a full row or column to clear it and score.

Chain combos

Clear 2 or more lines in one move for a growing COMBO bonus, or clear on consecutive moves for a streak multiplier.

Classic or Survival

Play at your own pace in Classic, or race a ticking bomb tile in Survival for extra pressure.

Leaderboards

Compare your best score with everyone else via Google Play Games or Game Center — one leaderboard per mode.
